Origin Story

Hi Stranger, I'm Cabbage Head🥬

This project was inspired by my trip to Japan in 2026, where I stumbled upon something that quickly became a highlight of my travels: omikuji, or fortunes.

They were everywhere! Whether sitting in boxes on the street or tucked away in temples, I never missed an opportunity to get one. The interesting part was, I didn't mind if I was tied to a positive or negative fate, or even if the paper told me that I should cover my mouth when I yawn because people notice it (yes, that was a real one!).

There was a magic in knowing that out of the many options, this exact fortune was the one I was meant to find.

I wanted to mirror this magic in Fortune From, Fortune Found - a place where strangers send good fortunes out into the world, hoping they'll find just the right person and brighten their day. And who knows what fortune might find you, too!

Hope you stick around,
~ Cabbage Head🥬
